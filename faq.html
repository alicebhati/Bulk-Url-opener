Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bulk URL Opener?

Bulk URL Opener is a free online tool that helps users open multiple URLs at once without manually opening every link.

How can I open multiple URLs at once?

Simply paste your website links into the URL box, one URL per line, and click the Open All button.

Is Bulk URL Opener free?

Yes, Bulk URL Opener is completely free and does not require any registration.

Are my URLs stored?

No, URLs are processed directly in your browser and are not stored.

Who can use this tool?

SEO professionals, developers, marketers, researchers and regular users can use Bulk URL Opener to save time.
